# Reading companion

The outline is a margin beside the conversation. Its memorable device is a blue
evidence spine: each extracted item sits beside its exact source quote.

Palette: paper #f8fafc, ink #17263c, muted #52647a, spine #2262b6,
rule #d5dfe9, alert #963b30. System sans handles controls; Georgia gives evidence
quotes a reading voice. No remote fonts, images, shadows, or dashboard cards.

Layout: left aligned, one column, compact controls above a continuous outline.

    Sidekick              Local only
    Conversation title
    capture status / progress
    Search…        Category
    Decisions (2)
    | Item title
    | “Exact source…”
    | View source    Copy
    …
    Local data controls

Review: a card grid or large statistic would compete with reading. The continuous
spine makes source provenance the hierarchy; category headings only organize it.
Controls wrap at 320px and focus rings remain explicit.
